# What are dogs made of?

Most of my dogs are "mixed breed", what we use to call mutts. For the dogs we've tested, here's a summary
of their makeup.


## Ghost
Ghost is a mashup of a lot of dog breeds but if you look at his picture you think "Siberian Husky"
Turns out that's not the largest percentage, Ghost is more "Chow Chow" that "Husky". Where this shows up
is in his curly tail and his **blue** tongue! Ghost weighs in at 60lbs, he's lean and fast. Ghost
sheds **a lot**, probably the reason he ended up in a shelter. No amount of brushing seems to help.
![GhostAncestry](/img/dogs/ghost-ancestry%20(cropped-small).png "Ghost")

## Suki
Suki has a bit of Husky & Chow Chow as well but her coloring comes from the "Tree Walking Coonhound".
Suki is a _hunter_, anything that moves is prey and her body count of chipmunks **and** squirrels
is well over 50, _while on a leash_. At 31lbs, she's fast and nimble.
![SukiAncestry](/img/dogs/suki-ancestry%20(cropped-resized).png "Suki")

## Athena
Athena is a "sturdy dog", it's clear she has some "Pit Bull" in here lineage, but the Australian Shepherd
shines through as Athena is very smart and expressive.
